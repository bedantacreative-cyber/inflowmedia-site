The Production

What We Build For You

01

Ideation & Scripting

We work with you to find the narrative worth telling — the arc, the hook, the proof points that make an investor or customer lean in. Every script is built before a camera rolls.

02

Storyboard Curation

Shot lists, visual references, and a full storyboard signed off before production day — so nothing is figured out on the fly and there's no room for surprises.

03

Full Production

Filming, direction, and on-site production handled end to end — whether that's a founder-led talking piece, a product walkthrough, or full motion graphics.

04

Edit & Color Grade

A polished, investor-grade cut — sound design, color grade, motion graphics, and pacing built for the specific moment it needs to land in.

05

Multi-Format Delivery

The main cut, plus a trailer, a short-form clip set, and a press-ready still — so one production covers the deck, the socials, and the homepage.

06

Launch-Day Support

We're on call the day it goes live — captions, timing, and platform-specific cuts ready to go the moment you hit publish.
