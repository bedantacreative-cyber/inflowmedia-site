FAQ

Common Questions

What's the minimum commitment?

3 months. Narrative consistency doesn't show up in investor conversations or customer decisions in 30 days — the system needs time to compound before it's worth judging.

Do I have to be on camera myself?

For founder-led content, yes — investors and customers respond to a real person, not a brand account. We handle everything around you: strategy, scripts, editing, and distribution.

How much of my time does this actually take?

Most founders spend under 2 hours a month — one recording session, one review call. We handle the strategy, scripting, editing, and posting around that.

Can you guarantee results?

No, and anyone who guarantees follower counts or inbound volume is selling you something. We can guarantee the strategy, the cadence, and a system that compounds — the same one behind our clients' organic growth.

What if I don't have a big audience yet?

That's exactly when narrative consistency matters most — you're building the story before you need it, not scrambling to write one mid-raise.
