FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Who is this actually for?

Founders and startup teams with a launch, a raise, or a product moment that needs to land — plus podcasters and creators sitting on unused footage. If you're pre-product or looking for a single one-off edit, this isn't the right fit.

Do you only work with funded startups?

No. We work with founders at any stage — bootstrapped, pre-seed, or funded. What matters is that you have a product or story worth telling well.

What's the commitment?

Launch projects are scoped as a single production sprint. Ongoing content systems (podcast, ongoing distribution) run on a 3-month minimum so the system has time to compound.

Do I need to be on camera myself?

For founder-led content, yes — investors and customers respond to a real person. We handle everything around you: scripting, shot planning, editing, and distribution.
