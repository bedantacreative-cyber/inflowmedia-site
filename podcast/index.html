The Process

From Recording
To Viral Clip

01
You Record
Record your episode as you normally would. Send us the raw file — video or audio only. We handle everything from there. No extra work on your end.
02
We Edit + Clip
Our editors go through the full episode, pull the 30 best moments, rewrite hooks, add captions, and format for every platform. Delivered in a shared Google Drive folder within 5 business days.
03
You Approve
We send you a content calendar with every clip scheduled. You review, give feedback, or approve. One round of revisions included.
04
We Distribute
We post everything according to the calendar — across Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok. Analytics tracked weekly. You see results by the end of month one.
FAQ

Common Questions

How fast do I get clips back?

5 business days from raw file to delivered clips, every episode — 30 short-form pieces plus a trailer, dropped in a shared Drive folder with a content calendar attached.

What's the minimum commitment?

3 months. Distribution compounds — we've never seen it work as a single-episode test, and we won't pretend it does to close a deal.

Do you handle guest booking or outreach?

No. We handle everything after you hit record — editing, clipping, and distribution. Guest booking and outreach stay with you or your team.

Can you guarantee views or growth?

No, and anyone who guarantees a number is selling you something. We can guarantee the editing quality, the clip volume, and the distribution cadence — the same system behind 50M+ organic views for other clients.

What if my recording quality isn't great?

We can work with most home setups. If something's a real dealbreaker — usually audio, not video — we'll flag it on the first review before it becomes 30 wasted clips, not after.
