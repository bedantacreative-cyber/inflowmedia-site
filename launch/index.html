Questions, answered.

What does Inflow do?

We create launch videos and ongoing founder content, from ideas and scripting to production and distribution.

What is a launch video?

A short film introducing your product, usually combining the founder’s story, product footage, and motion graphics.

Do I need to be on camera?

For founder-led launches, yes. We help with the script, preparation, and delivery.

Can you work with footage we already have?

Yes. We can build the video around existing footage, product recordings, and brand assets.

Can you help beyond launch day?

Yes. We create ongoing founder content to keep your product and perspective in front of your audience.
